ABOUT THE GUIDE

Technology should be easier to understand.

A practical, Australian-focused starting point for people who want to learn about information technology, improve their digital confidence and find reliable resources.

INDEPENDENTEDUCATIONALAUSTRALIA

OUR APPROACH

Useful first.
Jargon last.

Information technology touches almost everything: how we communicate, study, work, access services and keep our information safe. But finding a clear explanation or the right official resource can take more effort than it should.

Australian IT Guide brings together approachable introductions to common technology topics and links to Australian organisations that provide more detailed guidance.

What you'll find here

  • Plain-language introductions to IT concepts and infrastructure.
  • Practical starting points for online safety and cybersecurity.
  • Ideas for exploring IT roles, skills and learning pathways.
  • Links to official Australian government information and services.

What this site isn't

This is an independent educational resource, not a government website, an emergency service or a substitute for professional cybersecurity, legal or financial advice. External organisations own and maintain their linked content, and their policies can change.

For urgent account, financial or security incidents, contact the relevant organisation or official Australian service directly.
