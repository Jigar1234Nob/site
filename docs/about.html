WHAT I’M ABOUT

Many interests.
One thing in common.

I enjoy learning through hands-on projects. I’ll try a new idea, test it, work through the problems and improve it as I go. Not every experiment works first time, which is probably why troubleshooting has become part of the fun.

My interests include music, web and app development, IT, Linux and Windows systems, virtual machines, self-hosting, networking, hardware and community projects. I’m proud to be Australian, and this site brings my different interests together in one place.
